Export format
Day-wise goes to “<tab> Summary”: one row per day with wake, work and sleep times. Detailed goes to the tab above (slots) and “<tab> Review” (intentions, comments, summaries). Vision goals go to “<tab> Vision”, and “Export all days” also adds “<tab> Ideal”.

Daily backup
Once a day the app copies all your data to a “Planner Backup” tab in your Google Sheet (uses the Apps Script URL above). The previous copy is kept too, and every backup is read back to check it.

Safety copies
If a day was ever replaced or shortened (for example after editing on two devices), the older version is kept here so you can put it back.
Saved on the wrong date? Copy a whole day to the date it belongs to. The day you copy from is left as it is, and anything on the target date is kept as a safety copy first.
Ideal day
Your perfect-day template, used as a guide for every day.
Habits
Things you want to do again and again. They appear under your intentions each day, separately for Work and Life. Mark done, no, or skip. A skipped day does not break the streak.
Recurring slots
Something that happens on the same days every week, such as a stand-up or the gym. It is filled into those time slots automatically from today onward. You can still change or clear it on any day.
